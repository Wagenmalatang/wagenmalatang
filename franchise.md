# Franchise Partnership

Bring Wagen Malatang to your city. We partner with motivated entrepreneurs to
open new locations and share in our growth.

## Why Partner With Us?

- Proven business model
- Full training and onboarding support
- Marketing and branding support
- Ongoing operational guidance

## How to Apply

1. Submit an inquiry with your name, location, and investment budget
2. Schedule an introductory call with our franchise team
3. Review the franchise agreement and financial requirements
4. Complete training and prepare for opening
